Who We Are

Restoring Dignity & Opportunity

Divine Fatherhood Ministries International (DFMI) is a community-centered, faith-based organization established to address the spiritual, educational, health, and socio-economic challenges facing orphans and vulnerable families.

Our Mission

To provide holistic care through education, health support, spiritual guidance, skills development, and community empowerment for orphans and vulnerable children and families.

Our Vision

A transformed community where every child grows with dignity, opportunity, and strong spiritual values.

100%

Commitment to holistic care for body, mind, and spirit.

Why We Exist

DFMI was founded in response to the plight of orphans and vulnerable children in Luwero District, a region still recovering from the impact of the 1981–1986 civil war. Many children were left orphaned, homeless, and without access to basic education. Today, limited access to school requirements has contributed to a growing number of out-of-school youth and rising social challenges. We exist to restore hope, rebuild lives, and create pathways to education, skills, and dignity.
